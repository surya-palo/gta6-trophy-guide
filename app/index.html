    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough, and everything except one early meditation stays available after the story. Expect roughly 200 hours (PowerPyx) to 250–300+ hours (Push Square) for the platinum; difficulty about 4/10. Cheats disable trophies — and saving — so leave them alone.

    100% completion — the long pole

    The gold trophy A Long Road needs the 35-task 100% checklist, which opens after the story. PowerPyx estimates 100% is only about 60% of true completion. The big numbers: any 100 optional missions; 200 of 277 base-model vehicle scans; 100 of 255 animal studies; 15 of 18 meditation spots; 70 of 80 challenges; 300 of 397 stunts; 100 WAiNK scans; all robberies; all 8 bank vaults; all 39 gang operations; all underwater discoveries; all shooting-range golds; all drone races; all 15 Bret Target Decks.

    Missables

    Good news: GTA VI is a very forgiving platinum. There is only one trophy that can be missed, and it takes a minute to secure.

    Not missable — don't stress

    • All 12 story trophies unlock automatically and can never be missed.
    • Every stranger strand, questline, robbery, gang operation and collectible stays available in free roam after the story.
    • Kingpin Bowling's robbery respawns after a long time if you miss a valuable.
    • There is no mission replay, but nothing in the trophy list needs one.

    Save & export

    Your checklist, counters and notes are saved automatically in this browser (localStorage). Nothing leaves your device. Export a backup file before clearing browser data, and import it on another machine to pick up where you left off.
